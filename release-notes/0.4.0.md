Tabs and panes that move into each other, and a command line you can edit with the mouse.

## Highlights

- **Tabs into panes**: drag a tab over a terminal and drop it to make it a pane there. The half it will take is shown while you drag, picked by where the pointer is in the pane, and the tab keeps running, with all of its own panes. Rest the dragged tab on another tab for half a second to open it and drop it there. Over another window of Tanis Terminal the tab joins that window instead (on X11, Windows and macOS; on Wayland it still opens a new window) (#56).
- **Panes into tabs**: the menu of a pane in a split tab moves it to a new tab or a new window. With Alt held, drag a pane to another pane, to the tab bar (a new tab) or out of the window (#57).
- **Edit the command like a text field**: at the prompt of zsh and bash, click in the command to move the cursor there, and type, paste, Backspace or Delete over a selection in it to replace it. Typing clears any other selection (#62).
- **Agent usage**: Ctrl+Shift+A (Cmd+Shift+A on macOS) opens the usage screen and closes it (#58). Its sections show a vertical scrollbar (#61).
- **Window title**: the window takes the title of the active tab, so the taskbar and the window switcher name it (#60).

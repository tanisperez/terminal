Keyboard shortcuts

The shortcuts of Tanis Terminal use Ctrl Shift on Linux and Windows, because Ctrl alone belongs to the programs in the terminal, and Cmd on macOS. Every other key goes to the program.

Tabs

ActionLinux and WindowsmacOS
New tab with the default profileCtrl Shift TCmd T
Pick a profile for a new tabCtrl Shift SpaceCmd Shift Space
New tab with profile 1 to 9Ctrl Shift 1 to 9Cmd Option 1 to 9
Close the tabCtrl Shift WCmd W
Next tabCtrl Tab, Ctrl Page DownCtrl Tab, Cmd Shift ]
Previous tabCtrl Shift Tab, Ctrl Page UpCtrl Shift Tab, Cmd Shift [
